What changed

  1. Garland progress. A swag of C9 bulbs strung across the top. Each bulb switches on as more of the tree lights, so the garland doubles as a progress bar and all of it blazes on the win.
  2. Gilded wordmark. AGLOW keeps the Tracked layout but is set in gold foil, and a holly sprig (two leaves, three berries) replaces the dot. The berries glow as the tree lights.
  3. Ornament pills. Deep cranberry glass with a gold hairline and a warm top highlight replaces the grey glass. The timer gets a small bulb.
  4. Serif tagline. "Turn the wires. Light the tree." moves to gold Instrument Serif italic, which reads like a card rather than a hint.
  5. Presents under the tree. Wrapped gifts sit on the floor and catch the tree's light, dark when unlit and warming as it lights.
  6. Gift-wrap menu. A ribbon band and bow on the settings sheet, with gold selected states.
  7. Gift-tag results. The win card becomes a tag with a notched top, an eyelet and string, and a "Merry & bright" line. Gold and snow confetti fall on the win.
