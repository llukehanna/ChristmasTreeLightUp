Privacy

Updated October 7, 2026

You can play Aglow without an account. Signing in with Google is only for the leaderboard.

What is stored when you sign in

  • Your Google account ID and email address. Google shares these when you sign in (Aglow asks for nothing else). The ID keeps your account the same each time. The email must be verified by Google, and it is how the radio admin is recognised. Neither is ever shown to anyone.
  • Your display name. The name you pick is shown on the leaderboard next to your times. It is the only thing about you that is public.
  • Your games. For each tree: when it started and finished, your time, whether it ranked, and the log of your taps and pauses, which the server replays to check the time.
  • A sign-in session. A cookie keeps you signed in. Only a hashed copy of it is stored on the server.

What is stored when you play signed out

  • Your finished games, with no name attached, so you can still save them to the leaderboard by signing in on the same browser later.
  • A hashed copy of your IP address each time a game starts, used only to limit how many games one network can start in an hour. The address itself is never stored, and these records are deleted after an hour.
  • In your browser: the game in progress, your settings and local stats, and the keys that let you save signed-out games after you sign in.

How long it is kept

  • Your account, name and games: until you delete your account.
  • Your session: a year, renewed while you keep playing, or until you sign out.
  • Games played signed out: 90 days unless you sign in and save them. Games never finished: one day.

Who sees it

Everyone can see the leaderboard: display names, times and dates. Nothing else is public. Aglow runs on Cloudflare, which stores this data. It is never sold or shared, and there are no ads, analytics or tracking cookies. The pages load their fonts from Google Fonts, which sees your IP address as any website you visit does.

Deleting it

Sign in, open the menu under your name and choose Delete account. Your account, name, sessions and every saved game are deleted at once, for good.
